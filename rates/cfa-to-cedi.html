What the bank or forex bureau will actually give you

The number above is the mid-market rate, the middle point between what banks buy at and sell at. A bank or bureau in Accra sells CFA above it and buys below it, often by 3% to 6% each way; "black market" rates quoted on the street sit above the bank selling rate. Take the mid rate as the honest reference and expect to pay more when you buy CFA and receive less when you sell.

How much is 1,000 CFA to the cedi today?

1,000 XOF = GH₵ 20.03 on 19 September 2026 at the mid-market rate. Banks and forex bureaus sell CFA for more than this and buy for less.

Is the CFA higher than the cedi?

What is the Bank of Ghana rate?
