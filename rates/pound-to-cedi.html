What the bank or forex bureau will actually give you

The number above is the mid-market rate, the middle point between what banks buy at and sell at. A bank or bureau in Accra sells pounds above it and buys below it, often by 3% to 6% each way; "black market" rates quoted on the street sit above the bank selling rate. Take the mid rate as the honest reference and expect to pay more when you buy pounds and receive less when you sell.
