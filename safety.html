Is CountMy safe?

A plain answer, so you can check for yourself before you trust it.

🔒 Short answer

Your sales, spending, and customer records are saved on your own phone. Nobody at CountMy can see your name, your prices, or your customers' names unless you send us that yourself.

Where your records live

Every time you record a sale, expense, or a debt, it is saved directly on your phone first - the same as writing it in a notebook.

A safety copy is also kept so your records are never lost if your phone is lost, broken, or something is deleted by mistake. That copy is locked to your shop's own name, turned into a code nobody can read backwards - not even us.

Who can see your records

Only you, on your own phone. The safety copy can only be found again by someone who already knows your exact shop name - there is no list, no search, and no way to browse other people's shops.

What we never ask for

We never ask for your PIN, your bank details, or your Mobile Money PIN - not by phone, not by message, not ever. If anyone contacts you claiming to be CountMy and asks for these, it is not us.

What "free" actually means

CountMy costs nothing to use, with no signup. If you ever want to see your full history instead of just the last 7 days, that is 99 cedis once a year - entirely optional, and the free app keeps working either way.

Talking to the app

When you speak into CountMy, your words are sent once to understand what you said, then thrown away - they are not kept, listened to by a person, or used for anything else.
