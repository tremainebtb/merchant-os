Is CountMy safe?

A plain answer, so you can check for yourself before you trust it.

🔒 Short answer

Your records are saved on your own phone, and a safe copy is kept so they can be given back to you if your phone is lost. We do not read them, sell them, or share them with anyone. CountMy never touches your money.

Where your records live

Every time you record a sale, an expense, or a debt, it is saved on your phone first - the same as writing it in a notebook.

A safety copy is also kept so your records are never lost if your phone is lost, broken, or something is deleted by mistake. That copy is filed under your business name, so only someone who knows that name can ask for it back.

Who can see your records

You, on your own phone. The safety copy can only be found by someone who knows your exact business name - there is no list, no search, and no way to look through other people's records.

What we never ask for

We never ask for your Mobile Money PIN, your bank details, or any password - not by phone, not by message, not ever. If anyone contacts you claiming to be CountMy and asks for these, it is not us.

What "free" actually means

CountMy costs nothing to use, with no signup, and every part of it stays free. If it helps your business, you can choose to support it with 99 cedis once a year - entirely optional, and nothing changes if you do not.

Can CountMy take my money?

No. CountMy never touches money. It does not connect to Mobile Money, a bank, or a card. Nothing you record moves any money anywhere - it is a notebook, not a wallet.

Do I need Mobile Money or an account?

No. There is no account, no password, and no Mobile Money needed. Open countmy.app, tap the orange button, and speak. Talking needs a little data; typing works with no internet at all.

Talking to the app

When you speak into CountMy, your words are sent once to understand what you said, then thrown away - they are not kept, listened to by a person, or used for anything else.
