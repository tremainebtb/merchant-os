What each number means
Weekly active recorders. Different businesses that saved at least one record in the 7 days up to that day. The one number that says the notebook is being used.
New people. Real phones seen for the first time in the range. A wiped browser counts as a new phone.
Activation. Share of the new phones in the range that have saved at least one record.
Came back. Businesses active in the range on a day after their first day.
Habit. Businesses that recorded in the range and have recorded on two or more different days.
Cost per activated business. Ad spend in the range divided by the new phones from the ads that saved a record. Free visitors are left out, so the ads are not flattered. Spend comes from the ledger (read from Ads Manager).
Change vs previous. Against the same number of days just before the range, or the same dates a year earlier ("vs last year"). Before launch there is nothing to compare, so it says "no earlier data". Below 5 on either side it shows the raw change, because percentages on tiny numbers mislead.
* on a change. The previous range starts before launch, so it is only partly comparable.
Funnel. Only the phones that arrived in the range, followed to today.
