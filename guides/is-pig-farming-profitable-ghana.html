Is pig farming profitable in Ghana? How much does it cost to start

Pigs are the livestock people search about second, after chickens, and the honest answer is the same shape: the price you sell at is set by the associations and moves slowly; the price of feed moves every month and decides everything. This page gives the September 2026 feed prices, the live-weight price, the cycle, what the government offers, and a calculator for your own pigs.

The warning in the numbers: when maize rose from GH₵ 250 to GH₵ 360 a bag, 18 pig farms in Ashanti closed and the rest raised the live price to GH₵ 30 (GhanaWeb, above). Pork's selling price is set by an association meeting; feed's price is set by the market every month.

The cycle

  • A fattened pig is sold within about 6 to 8 months; a sow's gestation is about 114 days (One Africa Markets pig playbook, June 2025). The same playbook quotes GH₵ 5,000 to 20,000 to start; it gives no cost table.
  • No Ghanaian 2025 or 2026 breakdown for 5 or 10 pigs is published anywhere we could find. That is why the calculator asks for your own prices rather than printing a national one.
  • African swine fever, feed cost and capital are the three limits the Ashanti farm survey named, with the inability to keep records alongside them.

Feed Ghana

The Feed Ghana Programme (2025 to 2028) lists improved breeds, feed and vaccines for livestock farmers and training for cooperatives that includes record keeping, with concessional finance through Exim Bank (MoFA; GNA, Sep 2025). No subsidy percentage is published. Ask your district agriculture office what is actually on offer this season and write down what you were told and by whom.

Questions people ask

Is pig farming profitable in Ghana?

Only when feed per kilo of pig stays under GH₵ 30 a kilo of pig sold. The calculator above with your own bag count answers it for your pen.

How much does it cost to start a pig farm in Ghana?

A 2025 Ghanaian playbook quotes GH₵ 5,000 to 20,000. No published breakdown exists for a small pen; price the weaners and the bags in your own area.

How long before I sell?

About 6 to 8 months for a fattened pig.

What is the live-weight price?

GH₵ 30 a kg minimum, set by the farmers' associations in 2023 and 2024.

Why did farms close?

Feed. When maize went from GH₵ 250 to GH₵ 360 a bag, 18 Ashanti farms shut.
