How to pay tax in Ghana as a small business: which tax, how much, how to pay, and the dates

A shop, a chop bar, a seamstress, a driver, a farmer, a MoMo agent: the rules are the same and they are short. This page gives the 2026 income tax bands with a calculator, the three routes of the Modified Taxation Scheme, the tax stamp, when VAT touches you, how to register and pay, and the dates and penalties. Every figure links to GRA or a tax firm's published page.

Two things the calculator shows at once. First, at a small profit the bands give a small tax, because the first GH₵ 5,880 is free. Second, the 3% route is only cheaper when your profit margin is high; a shop that sells GH₵ 100,000 and keeps GH₵ 15,000 pays less on the bands than at 3%. Which route you can use depends on your sales figure, which is why the year's sales must be a number you can show.

The Modified Taxation Scheme, in one table

Live since 1 July 2025. GRA calls it "not an additional tax but a simplified method of calculating personal income tax" (GNA, July 2025).

RouteWhoWhat you pay
Presumptive Tax by Instalment (PTI)Average annual sales up to GH₵ 20,000 over three yearsA fixed quarterly amount by income level and type of business; GNA reports "up to GH₵ 45". GRA has not published the per-trade table; ask at the office.
Presumptive Tax on Turnover (PTT)Sales GH₵ 20,001 to 500,000Flat 3% of total sales, no expenses deducted
Modified Cash Basis (MCB)Sales GH₵ 20,001 to 500,000Bands on profit after allowable expenses

Excluded: qualified professionals, owners of several outlets, partnerships, and anyone who opts out. The tax stamp (GH₵ 3 to 45 a quarter) is still on GRA's site for the same informal trades; its range matches the PTI figure, but GRA has not said one replaces the other, so ask which you are on.

Do I have to charge VAT?

The Value Added Tax Act, 2025 raised the registration threshold from GH₵ 200,000 to GH₵ 750,000 of annual taxable turnover from 1 January 2026 for suppliers of goods. GRA has clarified that suppliers of services must register regardless of turnover (GhanaWeb, 2026). A market stall, a provision shop or a food seller is a seller of goods; a hairdresser or a mechanic sells a service and should ask GRA directly.

Register, file, pay

  1. Your Ghana Card number is your TIN. It replaced the separate TIN for individuals from 1 April 2021 (GRA). Companies keep the C-number.
  2. Register for the scheme with your Ghana Card, GhanaPost GPS address, phone number and basic sales information, through the MTS Taxpayer App, a GRA office, a GRA field officer or your trade association (GRA MTS page).
  3. File on taxpayersportal.com (sign up with the Ghana Card number and an OTP) or in the MTS app with your actual sales for the year.
  4. Pay by the *880# short code, a MoMo wallet linked in the MTS app, ghana.gov.gh, a bank, or at a GRA office. GRA does not publish the *880# menu; dial it and follow the prompts. Only the MoMo prompt on your phone should ever ask for your PIN.

Penalties on GRA's offences page (Revenue Administration Act, 2016): late return GH₵ 500 plus GH₵ 10 a day; failing to keep proper records, 75% of the tax for the period if deliberate, otherwise up to GH₵ 250; failing to register, the tax due plus a fine.

The record GRA expects

The scheme's year-end return asks for actual sales. Guidance from tax firms says: a daily sales book, an expense file if you use the cash basis, and your registration, returns and receipts (TaxLawGH, August 2026). Where there are no records, GRA assesses on an estimate, and an estimate is the one number you cannot argue with. Said out loud each day, the record writes itself: how to record daily sales.

Questions people ask

How much is income tax in Ghana?

0% on the first GH₵ 5,880 of annual chargeable income, then 5%, 10%, 17.5%, 25%, 30% and 35% by band. Non-residents pay a flat 25%.

How much is business tax in Ghana for a small trader?

Either the bands on profit, or under the Modified Taxation Scheme a flat 3% of sales (GH₵ 20,001 to 500,000) or a fixed quarterly amount (up to GH₵ 20,000 of sales).

How do I pay tax online in Ghana?

taxpayersportal.com or the MTS app to file; *880#, linked MoMo, ghana.gov.gh or a bank to pay.

Do Ghanaians pay income tax if they are self-employed?

Yes, above GH₵ 490 a month of chargeable income, by self-assessment.

What is the difference between the tax stamp and the Modified Taxation Scheme?

The tax stamp is a quarterly advance (GH₵ 3 to 45) for informal trades; the scheme is the simplified way of working out the year's income tax. GRA lists both; ask the office which applies to you.
