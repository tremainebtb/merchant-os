The practical answer to "how much do MoMo agents earn per month" is: your own record of the commission on each transaction, added up. An agent who says "commission 5" after each cash-out can check the monthly figure against a total of their own. An agent who does not is trusting the statement.

E-levy is gone

People still search "e levy is back". It is not. Parliament repealed the Electronic Transfer Levy on 26 March 2025 and the President assented on 2 April 2025 (Ghana Revenue Authority; Presidency). If a customer asks why the charge is what it is, the answer is the table above and nothing else.

The e-cash and cash problem

Agents call it float: the e-cash on the agent SIM plus the physical cash in the box. Every transaction must run against a pre-funded agent account, so no e-cash means no cash-in, and no cash means no cash-out. The problem is old and well described: it comes from too little working capital and from not being able to predict how much cash and e-cash the day will need; the remedy named in the same analysis is recording transactions so the pattern shows (Graphic Online, September 2022). Guides for new agents still list float shortage as the main struggle (Fido, April 2026).

The pattern is yours to see, if the days are written: which days cash-out runs ahead (salary week, market day, month end) and which days cash-in does. Two weeks of records tell you how much cash to carry on a Friday. No record, and every Friday is a surprise.

The scams that target agents

Fraud reports in Ghana rose 48% in 2025 to 24,778 cases, and mobile money and other digital platforms were more than 97% of them (Bank of Ghana fraud report, via Pulse, July 2026). Three patterns hit agents directly:

  1. The fake credit alert. A forged SMS that looks like a deposit, then a call begging for a "reversal" of money that never arrived. Rule: never pay out on a customer's screen or an SMS; only on the balance and notification on your own agent SIM (how the fraud works; the Bank of Ghana guideline on receipts, section 22).
  2. The PIN-prompt swap. An accomplice fires a withdrawal at the customer's number while the customer is at your point; the customer approves it thinking it is yours. MTN, Telecel and AT warned about it in June 2026 (Pulse, June 2026). Tell customers to read the prompt before approving.
  3. The physical one. Agents are robbery targets; two vendors were killed in Kumasi and Aflao in March and April 2025 (CISA newsletter, 2025). Keep less cash at the point than the record says you need, and bank the rest.

A written record does not stop a scam. It does show, within the hour, that GH₵ 500 left the box and nothing arrived on the SIM, which is when it can still be reported.

Questions agents ask

How much commission do MTN MoMo agents get?

MTN's help centre lists 1% between GH₵ 50 and GH₵ 2,000 and GH₵ 20 flat above; third-party guides quote 0.4% and GH₵ 8 flat from March 2024. Check the rate on your own monthly statement against your own count.

How do MoMo agents get paid?

Monthly, from MTN, as two commissions: one for cash-out and one for cash-in.

Is E-levy back?

No. Repealed 2 April 2025.

What are the cash-out charges in 2026?

GH₵ 0.50 up to GH₵ 50, 1% from GH₵ 50 to GH₵ 2,000, GH₵ 20 flat above. Cash-in is free.

How much do I need to start?

Guides for new agents quote GH₵ 3,000 to GH₵ 20,000 of starting cash and e-cash; registration itself is free. Those are guide estimates, not MTN figures.

Does CountMy connect to my agent SIM or MoMo?

No. It only keeps the record you say to it. It never asks for a PIN, a SIM or a password.
