Five subtractions. The owner fixes the first, the pump fixes the second, and the last three move every day. A driver who writes the five numbers each evening knows the day's real profit; a driver who does not is guessing at month end.

How the sales system works

A trotro driver does not earn a wage. The driver and mate run the car as a daily franchise: the owner demands a fixed daily fee, called sales, and the crew keeps what remains after sales and fuel. Owners do not contribute to fuel. Police, station and union dues, tolls and small repairs also come out of the crew's side, not the owner's (Humanities & Social Sciences Communications, 2020).

The sales figure does not move with the day. When the GPRTU cut fares by 15% in May 2025, drivers protested that owners kept sales the same: one driver reported "my car owner takes GH₵ 350 from me every day" (YEN, May 2025). Fares then rose 20% in June 2026 (MyJoyOnline, May 2026) and a further increase was being negotiated in mid September 2026. The fare moves; the sales figure is whatever you and the owner agreed.

Fuel, this window

Ghana's pump prices change on the 1st and 16th of the month. For the second September 2026 window the National Petroleum Authority's floor prices were petrol GH₵ 16.00 and diesel GH₵ 16.77 per litre (Ghana Eye Report, 16 Sep 2026); at the pump GOIL listed Super XP at GH₵ 16.64 and Diesel XP at GH₵ 18.46 from 17 September (GOIL). The chief executive of the Chamber of Bulk Oil Distributors put a Madina to Circle trotro at about 25 litres a journey (Daily Graphic, 18 Sep 2026).

Own arithmetic, not a reported figure: 25 litres × GH₵ 18.46 is about GH₵ 460 of diesel for that one journey. Whatever your route, write the litres and the cedis each time you fill; two weeks later the price changes and your record is the only thing that shows what it did to you.

Where the money leaks

  1. Chobo. Takings that never reach the count. Filmed cases of mates dividing the day's money are common enough to have a name (YEN, Dec 2024). A driver who says "Trip Madina Circle 90" as each trip ends has a count that does not depend on anyone's memory.
  2. Police and fines. The Concerned Drivers Association said in August 2026 that drivers spend "almost half" of revenue on police extortion (Adom, Aug 2026). From 1 October 2026 camera fines arrive by SMS (Daily Graphic, Sep 2026). Write every one on the day it happens, with the place.
  3. Chop money mixed with car money. The MoMo and GhIPSS campaign for drivers in July 2026 said it in two lines: keep proper transaction records, separate personal and business money (High Street Journal, Jul 2026). "Chop money 30" on its own line keeps the car's profit true.
  4. Repairs paid from memory. Tyres, oil and servicing come out of the day's money weeks after the day. If they are not written, the month looks better than it was.

Questions drivers ask

What is "sales" for a trotro driver?

The fixed daily amount paid to the car owner, whatever the day brought in. The crew keeps what is left after sales, fuel, dues and repairs.

How much does a trotro driver make a day?

No single figure exists, and anyone who gives one is guessing. The reported pieces above (sales of GH₵ 350 for one owner, mates at GH₵ 100 to 200) show the range; your own written days are the only true answer.

How much does Bolt take?

Bolt publishes 20% of the final price. Drivers' groups reported deductions up to 30% on some platforms in 2025 and 2026.

Should I count chop money as a car cost?

No. Write it on its own line. Otherwise the car looks less profitable than it is, and you cannot see which of the two is the problem.

Do I need to write the passengers or only the money?

Money is enough for profit. Passengers per trip help you argue about the fare and about chobo; say them if you can.
