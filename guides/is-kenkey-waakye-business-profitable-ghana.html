One reported day

A Kumasi kenkey seller who chose the trade over travelling abroad told YEN in February 2026: "On a good day, I can make GH₵ 500 as profit" (YEN, Feb 2026). It is one seller, self-reported, on a good day, with no cost breakdown. We quote it because it is the only real figure published this year, and we do not multiply it by 30.

Permits

In Accra the AMA requires every cook, server and food seller to hold a Food Handlers' Certificate; the last published fee was GH₵ 60 plus GH₵ 60 for the medical screening, covering TB, typhoid and hepatitis A (AMA, Aug 2023). No 2026 fee is published for Accra or Kumasi; ask the Assembly and write down what you paid, because it is a business cost for the year.

Questions people ask

Is kenkey business profitable in Ghana?

One seller reports GH₵ 500 on a good day. Yours depends on your own prices against a GH₵ 5 ball; the calculator above and a nightly count answer it for you.

Is ice kenkey business profitable?

No published price or margin exists for ice kenkey in 2026. Count your cups, your kenkey, your milk and sugar and your ice for one week and you will know more than any page.

How much is a ball of kenkey?

GH₵ 4 to 8 in Accra; most people pay GH₵ 5.

How much is a plate of waakye?

About GH₵ 15 to 35 at street and chop-bar level in Accra, more at tourist spots.

What permit do I need to sell food?

A Food Handlers' Certificate from your Assembly with a medical screening. Ask the current fee.
