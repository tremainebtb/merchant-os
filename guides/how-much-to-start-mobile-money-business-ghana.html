How much do you need to start a mobile money business in Ghana? Is it profitable?

Ghana had 491,057 active mobile money agents in 2025, so the question is not whether the business exists; it is whether the next one, yours, pays. None of these are MTN figures; they are the authors' estimates, and they agree with each other within a range. The float is not a cost; it is capital you must have and must not need for anything else, because the moment it runs out you cannot serve the next customer.

Is it profitable? What is actually known

  • No Ghanaian news feature in 2025 or 2026 quotes an agent's monthly commission. The guides say "profitable" without a number. Treat every number you see elsewhere as an estimate.
  • Agents are many: MTN alone reports over 42,000 agents and Telecel Cash about 18,000 (JBKlutse, May 2026); the Bank of Ghana counts 491,057 active agents across all networks in 2025 (BoG report via MyJoyOnline, Sep 2026). Your location, not the commission table, decides your volume.
  • The agents' associations have raised network outages, security of investments, fraud and SIM availability with the Minister for Communication (MoC, Sep 2025), and one association is urging agents to add other services as digital competition rises (Rainbow Radio, Sep 2026).
  • Fraud and robbery are the risks that turn a profitable month into a loss in an afternoon; the three patterns and the rule for each.

Questions people ask

How much do I need to start a mobile money business in Ghana?

Guides quote GH₵ 4,000 to 10,000 of cash float plus the same in e-cash, and GH₵ 1,000 to 5,000 for the structure. Registration is free.

Is mobile money business profitable?

No published agent earnings exist for 2025 or 2026. Commission is 0.4% on most cash-outs, GH₵ 8 above GH₵ 2,000, plus an unpublished cash-in rate. Your volume decides.

How does mobile money business work?

You hold cash and e-cash. A cash-out gives the customer cash and moves e-cash to you; a cash-in is the reverse. MTN pays commission monthly on both.

Can I run it from home?

The guides say no: MTN requires a permanent physical structure with signage and inspects it.

What is the commission liquidation code?

"Liquidating" moves earned commission from the commission balance to your main wallet. MTN Ghana has not published the menu path; the *502# code found online is Nigeria's. Dial the agent menu on your agent SIM and follow it.
