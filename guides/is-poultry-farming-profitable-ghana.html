Is poultry farming profitable in Ghana? How much do you need to start

Ghana eats about 400,000 tonnes of chicken a year and grows about 15,000 of it; the rest is imported. That is the demand. The question is whether a batch of 50, 100 or 500 birds pays after feed, which is about 70% of the cost and rose again this month. This page gives the September 2026 prices with their sources, the two published cost sheets, what the government programmes actually give, and a calculator for your own batch.

Eggs: the price that goes both ways

A crate of 30 sold at the farm gate for GH₵ 40 to 45 and in the market for GH₵ 70 to 75 in November 2025, with about 38,000 crates unsold on farms as farmers accused market women of holding prices up (MyJoyOnline, Nov 2025). When Burkina Faso restricted egg imports in early 2026 a crate fell to GH₵ 50 to 55 at Koforidua market (Citi, Apr 2026); the trade reopened in July 2026 (Citi, Jul 2026). A layer farm's month is decided by who buys the crates and at what price, which is why the crate count and the price per crate belong in the same line of the record.

The import problem, in numbers

  • Ghana consumes about 400,000 tonnes of chicken a year and produces about 15,000 tonnes locally, roughly 4.6%; the import bill is about $400 million a year (Agriculture Minister via Graphic, Nov 2025; Poultry News Africa, Nov 2025).
  • Imports carry a 35% tariff plus VAT and levies, and frozen imports still land far below the local cost of a bird (USDA GAIN 2024). No import ban is in force in 2025 or 2026; the government says restriction must be gradual.
  • Target: 75% self-sufficiency by 2028 (GNA, Nov 2025).

The batch record

The half of poultry that pages cannot answer is the half you can: how many chicks arrived, how many died each week, how many bags went in, what the buyer paid per bird. A 2010 study of small poultry farmers in Ga East found the main reason for not keeping detailed records was that farmers saw no benefit in it (Journal of Agricultural Science, 2010); the Ashanti pig-farm survey lists the inability to keep records among the factors holding farms back. The benefit is the calculator above filled with true numbers instead of placeholders.

Questions people ask

Is poultry farming profitable in Ghana?

The published sheets show 14% to 27% on 1,000 broilers at 2022 to 2023 prices. Feed has risen since. Yours is the calculator above with your numbers.

How much do you need to start poultry farming in Ghana?

About GH₵ 63 a broiler for chick and feed at September 2026 prices, before housing, heat, vaccines and labour. Chicks are sold in lots of 50.

How much does it cost to start a poultry farm in Ghana?

For 1,000 broilers the 2023 USDA sheet totalled GH₵ 98,375 for one cycle. Scale it by your birds and today's feed price.

How many weeks to market?

7 to 8 weeks to about 2.5 kg live in the published models.

What is a crate of eggs selling for?

GH₵ 40 to 55 at the farm gate over the last year, GH₵ 70 to 75 in the market in November 2025. It moves with gluts and border decisions.
