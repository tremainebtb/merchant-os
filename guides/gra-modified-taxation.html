What the scheme is

The Modified Taxation Scheme is GRA's simplified route for small and informal businesses: shops, market traders, artisans, drivers, food sellers. Instead of full accounts, you register with your Ghana Card, keep a simple daily record, and file a simplified return declaring your actual sales for the year, by 30 April. GRA staff have been taking the scheme to the markets themselves (Abeka-Lapaz traders were visited in December 2025), so it is being enforced from the market side, not only from the office.

The bands

Your band depends on your annual sales, which GRA calls turnover. From GRA's scheme page and the guides that summarise it:

Annual sales (turnover)BandWhat you pay
Up to GH₵ 20,000Small (presumptive)A fixed amount set by GRA for the band, not a percentage. Ask GRA or your association for the current figure.
GH₵ 20,001 to GH₵ 500,000Modified (turnover based)3% of your annual sales.
Above GH₵ 500,000Out of the schemeNormal income tax on accounts.

Two things follow from that table. First, whether you are in the scheme at all depends on one number, your annual sales. Second, at 3%, every GH₵ 100 you cannot prove you did not sell costs you GH₵ 3. A trader whose book is in her head is the trader who pays on a guess.

Quick sums at 3%: sales of GH₵ 30,000 a year → GH₵ 900 tax. GH₵ 60,000 → GH₵ 1,800. GH₵ 120,000 → GH₵ 3,600. Your real figure is the total of your daily sales, not what you remember in April.

The daily sales book GRA expects

The guides to the scheme describe a daily sales book that records sales however they were paid: cash, mobile money, bank, credit. Credit means a sale counts on the day the goods left, not the day you were paid. A book that only has cash gives a year figure that is too low, and "too low" is the one thing you do not want to argue with GRA about.

How to pay with *880#

GRA's pages describe payment through the short code. In summary:

  1. Register first: you need your Ghana Card, your GhanaPost GPS address, a phone number and basic information about your sales.
  2. Dial *880# on the phone linked to your MoMo.
  3. Choose Modified Taxation.
  4. Enter your Ghana Card number.
  5. Enter the amount and confirm with your MoMo PIN on the MoMo prompt.

Keep the confirmation message. Note that CountMy never asks for your Ghana Card or MoMo PIN; only the MoMo payment prompt on the *880# step does, and that is MTN's or Telecel's screen, not a website.

Do I have to charge VAT?

From 1 January 2026 the VAT registration threshold is GH₵ 750,000 of annual sales (up from GH₵ 200,000). Almost every market trader and small shop is far below it. Being in the Modified Taxation Scheme and charging VAT are different things; most people on this page will never touch VAT.

Questions people ask

What is the GRA Modified Taxation Scheme?

GRA's simplified income tax route for small and informal businesses, with bands by annual sales, a simplified return by 30 April, and payment through *880#.

What is my annual sales figure?

Everything you sold in the year, cash, MoMo, bank and credit added together. GRA calls it turnover.

I only sell small small. Do I need to register?

The scheme's lowest band starts at zero; GRA is registering traders in the markets. Ask at the GRA office nearest you or your trade association what applies to your size, and keep your daily record either way so the answer is a number, not a guess.

Do I have to charge VAT?

Only above GH₵ 750,000 of annual sales from 1 January 2026.

What if I lost last year's book?

Your MoMo statement gives the MoMo part. For cash you will estimate, and the estimate is what you will pay on. Start the daily record today so next year is not an estimate.
