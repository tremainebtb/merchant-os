If the drawer has GH₵ 150, something happened today, and you will find it tonight, not in three weeks.

The three mistakes that ruin the book

  1. Chop money written as a cost. Money you took home for food is not a shop cost. Write it on its own line ("took home 30") so your profit stays true. CountMy keeps it separate automatically.
  2. Stock bought written as a cost of the day. GH₵ 2,000 of rice bought today is not "lost" today; it is on your shelves. Write it as stock, not as an expense, or every buying day looks like a disaster.
  3. Credit sales left out. The rice Ama took is a sale today. Leave it out and your sales are too low, your "who owes me" is in your head, and your GRA figure is wrong. How to keep the customer book.

Cash versus MoMo

Most market sales are still cash; MoMo is growing and its charges are a cost that traders forget to write. Two rules: write the way you were paid on every line, and at closing count the drawer against the cash column. The MoMo column you can check on your phone; the cash column only your book can check.

In CountMy you say how you were paid in the same breath: "Sold oil 85, MoMo." The day card shows cash and MoMo separately, and the drawer count becomes a one-glance check.

What GRA expects

Under the Modified Taxation Scheme, small businesses keep a daily sales book covering cash, mobile money, bank and credit sales, and declare actual sales for the year by 30 April. The five-column page above is that book. Add the days up and you have the annual figure.

Questions people ask

What should a daily sales record contain?

What was sold, how many, the cedis, how you were paid, and costs on their own line. Total at the bottom.

Is chop money a business expense?

No. It is money you took home. Write it, separately.

How do I record a sale on credit?

As a sale today, with the customer's name and the day she promised. It goes into your sales total and onto her page.

How do I keep a sales record without writing?

Say each sale to your phone. CountMy is free and writes it for you.

What if I forget a day?

Write what you remember that evening and mark it "estimate". A day marked estimate is better than a blank page and better than a wrong number you later believe.
